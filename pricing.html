Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Can I upgrade or downgrade later?

Yes, anytime. Upgrade to Pro and you'll be charged pro-rated for the remainder of your billing cycle. Downgrade and we'll credit the unused portion to your next bill.

What payment methods do you accept?

Pro tier: Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe. Enterprise: Credit cards, ACH, wire transfer, or invoicing (net-30 terms available).

Is there a refund policy?

Yes. 30-day money-back guarantee for Pro tier. If you're not satisfied, contact us within 30 days of your first payment for a full refund.

What happens to my proofs if I downgrade?

Nothing! All your existing proofs remain valid and public. You just lose access to Pro/Enterprise features for new proofs. Your verification pages stay live forever.

Do you offer non-profit discounts?

Yes! Registered non-profits get 50% off Pro tier. Contact us at hello@truwit.ai with proof of non-profit status (501(c)(3) or equivalent).

Can I try Pro before buying?

Pro tier will have a 14-day free trial when it launches (Q2 2025). Enterprise customers can request a personalized demo anytime at sales@truwit.ai.
