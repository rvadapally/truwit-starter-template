Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What's your response time?

We aim to respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please mark your subject as "Urgent" or call our support line (coming soon).

Do you offer demos?

Yes! For enterprise customers and investors, we're happy to schedule a personalized demo. Contact us at sales@truwit.ai to set up a time.

Are you hiring?

We're a small but growing team. If you're passionate about building infrastructure for digital truth, reach out to hello@truwit.ai with your background and why you'd like to join us.

Can I get a custom solution?

Absolutely. Our Enterprise tier includes white-label solutions and custom integrations. Contact sales@truwit.ai to discuss your specific requirements.
