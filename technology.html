Cryptographic Security

Military-grade algorithms protecting your content

SHA-256 Hashing

What it does: Creates a unique "fingerprint" of your content. Even a single pixel change produces a completely different hash.

Security level: Same algorithm used by Bitcoin and NSA-approved for classified documents.

Example hash:
a1b2c3d4e5f6a7b8c9d0e1f2a3b4c5d6e7f8a9b0c1d2e3f4a5b6c7d8e9f0a1b2

Ed25519 Signing

What it does: Creates a digital signature that proves the hash hasn't been tampered with.

Security level: 128-bit security. Breaking it would require more computing power than exists on Earth.

Impossible to fake: Only our private key can create valid signatures. Even quantum computers can't break it (yet).

Security & Privacy

Your data, your control

✓ What We Store

  • Content hash (NOT the content itself)
  • Metadata you choose to attach
  • Cryptographic signature
  • Timestamp of proof creation

✗ What We DON'T Store

  • The actual media files (deleted after hashing)
  • Personal information (unless you add it)
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • IP addresses or browser fingerprints

Privacy by Design

We can't read your content. The hash is a one-way function—mathematically impossible to reverse. Even if our database was compromised, attackers would only see random strings of characters.

Think of it like this: We're storing your content's DNA, not your content. The DNA proves it's yours, but doesn't contain the actual data.
