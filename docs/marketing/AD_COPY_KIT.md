## 💬 Objection Handlers

**"Why should I trust you?"**
> You don't have to. The proof is anchored to Bitcoin - verify it yourself at opentimestamps.org.

**"Is this NFTs?"**
> No. No buying, no selling, no speculation. Just proof you made something.

**"Will this hold up in court?"**
> Cryptographic timestamps are increasingly accepted as digital evidence. Combined with our RFC 3161 integration (coming soon), you'll have strong legal standing.

**"What if TruWit shuts down?"**
> Your .ots file is self-contained and verifiable against the Bitcoin blockchain forever. We're just making it easy to create - the proof lives independently.
