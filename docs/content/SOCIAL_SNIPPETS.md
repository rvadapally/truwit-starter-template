## Reddit Comments (Helpful, Non-Promotional)

### When someone asks "How do I protect my art from AI?"

> Honestly? You can't fully prevent scraping anymore. But you CAN create proof you made it first.
> 
> Look into cryptographic timestamping - it creates a mathematical fingerprint of your work anchored to a permanent ledger. When theft happens (and it will), you have evidence for takedowns.
> 
> Watermarks get removed. Metadata gets stripped. But a cryptographic proof? That's forever.

---

### When someone complains about AI art theft

> This is why I timestamp everything before posting now. Not that it prevents theft, but when I need to file a DMCA or dispute something, I have cryptographic proof I had it first.
> 
> The proof is anchored to Bitcoin's blockchain so it can't be faked or backdated. Sounds techy but it takes like 10 seconds.

---

### When someone asks about proving they made something

> For proof of creation timing, look into OpenTimestamps. It's a protocol that anchors a hash of your file to Bitcoin's blockchain.
> 
> You keep your file private - only the hash (a fingerprint) is public. Anyone can verify the timestamp later. No crypto wallet needed, and it's free because many timestamps get batched into one transaction.

---

### When discussing NFTs negatively

> I hear you on NFTs - the gas fees and crypto complexity killed it for most artists.
> 
> There's simpler tech now that just does the timestamp part without the marketplace stuff. OpenTimestamps has been around since 2016, anchors to Bitcoin, and costs nothing because it batches timestamps together.
> 
> You get proof you had the file at a certain time. No wallet, no gas, no speculation.
