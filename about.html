Our Mission

Truwit was founded on the belief that truth shouldn't be negotiable. As AI makes it trivially easy to create fake content, we're building the infrastructure to prove what's real.

Our vision is a world where every piece of content can be cryptographically verified, restoring trust in the digital age. We're not just building a product—we're building the foundation for digital truth.
